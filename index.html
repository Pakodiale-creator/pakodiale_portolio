P-01

Heart Disease Prediction Model

Built a supervised machine learning model to predict the presence of heart disease from demographic and clinical variables. Cleaned and preprocessed the dataset, ran exploratory analysis using correlation heatmaps and count plots, applied feature scaling with an 80/20 stratified train-test split, and evaluated performance using accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score and a confusion matrix — reaching 80.3% prediction accuracy.

P-02

Air Pollution Clustering Analysis

Analysed an air quality dataset covering PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, SO₂ and O₃ measurements to uncover pollution patterns. Normalised variables with StandardScaler, applied K-Means clustering, and used the Elbow Method and Silhouette Score to determine the optimal number of clusters — identifying three distinct pollution profiles linked to particulate matter and traffic-related emissions.
